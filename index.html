Roundcube shipped security releases 1.6.19 and 1.7.4 on September 6, fixing 12 vulnerabilities including a zero-click stored XSS bug, an SSRF bypass, and several email-header injection flaws. The project's lead maintainer urged all production installations to update immediately.

Dropbox breach via Lenovo ID flaw exposed ~5,000 accounts

Dropbox confirmed attackers exploited a flaw in Lenovo's federated login to register a Lenovo ID under a victim's email and sign in without ever touching their Dropbox password. Dropbox invalidated all Lenovo-linked sessions and now requires a Dropbox password even for federated sign-in.
