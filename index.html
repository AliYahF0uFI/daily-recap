Anthropic will watermark text from new Claude models

Anthropic said Claude models released after August 2 automatically embed an imperceptible watermark into generated text and files, a move tied partly to the EU AI Act's transparency rules that took effect that day. Zoom patches zero-click 'Zoomsday' flaw

Zoom patched CVE-2026-53413, a critical zero-click remote-code-execution bug in its annotation feature that could let one meeting participant silently take over every other participant's device with no warning. Researchers said the flaw was discovered and weaponized using publicly available AI models in under 24 hours, and no in-the-wild exploitation had been reported as of disclosure.
CybernewsSecurity

ShinyHunters claims Metabase breach after critical zero-day

Extortion group ShinyHunters listed analytics platform Metabase on its dark-web leak site days after Metabase disclosed a maximum-severity SQL injection zero-day that let unauthenticated attackers gain admin access to instances. The leak-site post did not detail what data was taken, but the underlying flaw had already exposed customer data at companies including Framework and Tally.

Nvidia open-sources Nemotron 3.5 Lightning

Nvidia released Nemotron 3.5 Lightning, a free, open-source 30-billion-parameter mixture-of-experts model with only 3 billion active parameters per token, designed to run agent workloads on a single GPU. It shipped alongside NeMo Switchyard, an open-source router that directs agent requests to the most cost-efficient model, with weights available on Hugging Face, ModelScope, and OpenRouter.
